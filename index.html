FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are live in under 10 minutes. Connect GitHub, Jira, or Linear; pick your north-star metric; you're done. Axis auto-discovers the rest and builds your first dashboard before your coffee is cold.

Do you train AI on my data?

No. Your data is never used to train any model, ours or anyone else's. We offer private deployments for teams that need them, and we'll put that commitment in your DPA.

Can I export everything?

Yes. A one-click export gives you every dashboard, decision log, and metric series as CSV or JSON. We believe in easy exits — lock-in is not a strategy.

What integrations are supported?

60+ and counting: GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Linear, Slack, Notion, Figma, Sentry, Datadog, Mixpanel, Amplitude, BigQuery, Snowflake, and more. If we don't have it, ask — we ship one new connector most weeks.

Is there a self-hosted version?

On Scale and Enterprise plans, yes. Axis ships as a single Helm chart and runs happily on any Kubernetes 1.24+ cluster. Most teams keep it on our managed cloud and add a VPC peering for sensitive data sources.
